## Art direction

Cream polar bears act as thoughtful builders. Rust folded-paper architecture connects deep sage floating islands. Keep hand-ink outlines, paper grain, generous silhouette and restrained route nodes. Avoid technology clichés, rendered plastic, glow, letters and decorative badges.

Display at the original 3:2 ratio, with `object-fit: contain`; do not crop the paws or floating platforms. Paper, bone and ink backgrounds work well. The PNG masters and exact generation prompts in `prompts.json` are retained for future variants. Artwork is a flat bitmap; animate a containing layer or add independent route overlays rather than implying it includes separate rigged parts.
