# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and operational leaders at growing Calgary and Canadian organizations. They need clearer customer acquisition, stronger conversion, less manual administration, and better visibility across disconnected systems.

## Product Purpose

Explain and demonstrate how Arctos connects marketing, customer experience, software, automation, and reporting. Success means a qualified visitor understands the connected offer quickly, trusts the studio's judgement, and starts a useful project conversation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, and inventive. Intelligent without sounding academic, technically strong without becoming cold, premium without becoming distant.

## Anti-references

Generic AI SaaS templates, cyber-security dashboards, aerospace imagery, crypto aesthetics, outdoor-brand mountain imagery, novelty component showcases, and portfolio sites that value spectacle over clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Show the connected system, not a list of disconnected services.
2. Practice what Arctos sells through clear structure, strong conversion paths, and thoughtful technology.
3. Make every section a distinct chapter while preserving one visual grammar.
4. Use motion to explain sequence and causality.
5. Keep claims specific, honest, and useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard navigation, strong focus treatment, sufficient contrast, semantic structure, accessible forms, and complete reduced-motion behaviour.
