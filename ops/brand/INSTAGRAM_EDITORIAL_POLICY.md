# CalgaryDaily editorial policy

This is the complete posting program. Do not draft, approve or publish any
other format.

## 1. What's happening this week

Once a week. Pick the strongest verified Calgary events and markets in the
next seven days. Use a multi-slide Reel or carousel, not a plain text card.
Every date and time must come from the organizer's current source.

## 2. Best ___ in Calgary

At most once a day. Choose one useful category such as bakeries, coffee shops,
ramen, florists, patios, auto detailers or dog groomers. Rank ten Calgary
businesses from 1 (best) to 10 using current public review evidence. Record the
rating, review count, source and retrieval date for every business. Weight both
rating and review volume; never invent or estimate missing data and never sell
a ranking position.

The post is a carousel. Slide 1 is the category cover. Slide 2 contains the
complete ranking, 1 through 10, with 1 clearly marked best. Later slides may
show sourced details or relevant licensed/supplied business imagery. End the
caption with: `Do you agree?`

## 3. Major Calgary news

At most two posts in any rolling seven days. Use only a genuinely major,
Calgary-relevant headline from credible sources. Use a relevant licensed,
officially supplied or permission-cleared image; never publish a generic text
card or an invented illustration. State the source plainly.

## Hard rules

- Everything else stops: no daily Today/Tonight posts, individual event
  spotlights, date-night posts, standalone markets, opinions, reposts or filler.
- No ranking without ten sourced businesses and current review evidence.
- No post without a relevant visual asset.
- Facts, rankings and images must carry their sources in the draft.
